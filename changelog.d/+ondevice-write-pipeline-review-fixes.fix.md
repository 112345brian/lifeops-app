A follow-up review of the chore write-back and YNAB write pipeline caught and fixed a real money-duplication bug: the YNAB "cover" write applied a destination category's credit before its funding source's debit, so a tick that failed partway through could leave a category permanently holding uncredited money on retry — now debits always write before credits. Also fixed: an oversized `[cycle:Nd]` tag could crash the entire compute tick instead of being skipped as malformed, the chore write-back's error handling was narrower than intended (letting an unrelated failure abort gym/social/attention compute too), and the periodic and manually-triggered compute ticks had no protection against running concurrently. All fixed, with regression tests.
